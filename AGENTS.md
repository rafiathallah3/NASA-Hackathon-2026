# Project Context: ORBITAL KITCHEN (2.5D Lunar Sample Return)

## Technical Constraints
- **Zero Dependencies:** Use pure HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, and native browser ES modules (`<script type="module">`). Do NOT initialize `npm` or create `package.json` or `node_modules`.
- **4-Player Multiplayer Architecture:** Support 4 NASA segment roles (**P1 Launch**, **P2 Space**, **P3 Science**, **P4 Ground**) via role switching (`1`-`4`), Local 2P (`WASD` + `IJKL`), AI squadmates, and `BroadcastChannel` (`orbital_kitchen_4p_sync`).
- **Rendering:** Maintain crisp `devicePixelRatio` scaling on a logical `1120x620` canvas with a 3-zone non-overlapping flex viewport (Top Overcooked HUD, Center Canvas, Bottom Telemetry Strip). Keep sub-stage HUD badges (`STAGE 1B`, `STAGE 2`, `STAGE 3A`, `STAGE 3B`, `STAGE 3C`, `STAGE 4`).
- **Pre-game flow (`src/main.js`):** `PRE_GAME_STEPS` (`title` -> `lobby` -> `setup`) is an ordered list of screens; each `show(next, goTo)` renders one screen and calls `next()` to advance (`goTo(id)` jumps, e.g. Leave -> `title`), and running off the end calls `startGame(false)`. Insert new wireframe screens into that array. `openMenu({ title: true })` runs the full sequence (first boot only); Pause / Stage 3A-4 abort / Stage 5 menus enter at `setup`. Each screen tags `#modal-content` with its own class (`title-splash-card`, `lobby-card`); `clearPreGameScreens()` runs before every step and in `startGame`, so nothing else inherits that styling.
- **Splash screen (`TITLE_SCREEN_HTML` in `src/hud.js`, `TITLE / SPLASH SCREEN` block in `styles.css`):** full-bleed attract screen. `showTitleScreen` tags `#modal` with `.title-splash-open` and `#modal-content` with `.title-splash-card`; `hideTitleScreen` removes both. Backdrop = the game's layered space background (`Assets/background/layered/*`) + seeded twinkle stars + a looping liftoff rocket (`Assets/Vehicle/title-rocket.png`, a hard-alpha downscale of the Apollo sheet) + marching hazard tape; animations use `steps()` and honour `prefers-reduced-motion`. `Assets/lunar-mission-logo.png` is a transparent cut-out of `Assets/lunar-mission.jpeg` (own logo, already says "created by CKC"; keep the JPEG as the source). `Assets/nasa-space-app.png` is the OFFICIAL NASA Space Apps mark: NEVER redraw, recolour, filter/invert, pixelate or re-create it (no SVG imitations). It is black on transparent, so it sits on its own small light chip (`.ts-nasa`) with `image-rendering: auto` and its native 692:718 ratio.
- **Crew Lobby (`src/lobby.js`, `src/lobby-ui.js`):** presentation only, NO networking. `lobby.js` is the pure model (6-char room code, 4 seats P1 Launch / P2 Space / P3 Science / P4 Ground, host always ready, guests must ready up, empty seats auto-filled by deterministic BOTs); `joinGuest` / `leaveSlot` / `setReady` are the hooks a future network layer calls (try `__orbitalLobby.join({ name: 'Zed' })` then `__orbitalLobby.setReady(1, true)` in the console to see the host-waits flow). Start commits `state.crew = [{ index, role, kind, callsign, look }]`; the local `{ name, look }` persists in `localStorage` (`orbital_kitchen_profile_v1`). Stages dress sprites from `state.crew` (`p.callsign` is the tag text; `p.name` stays the `P1..P4` identity key used by game logic, never overwrite it).
- **Astronaut customization (`src/avatar-data.js`, `src/avatar.js`):** a look is `{ skin, hair (-1 = bald), hairColor, badge, helmet }` (indices into `SKIN_TONES` / `HAIR_STYLES` / `HAIR_COLORS` / `BADGES`). `avatar.js` recolours the stock bald `Character_*.png` sheets (skin = orange hue band, shading kept) and layers `Hair/`, `Helmet/` and `Badges/Badge.png` using the measured rects and placement tables in `avatar-data.js` (head boxes per frame, per-facing offsets; hair side art faces LEFT, helmet side art faces RIGHT). `getCrewSheet(look, sheetKey)` returns a cached 8x2 sheet for `drawChibiEngineer` (frames carry `AVATAR_HEADROOM` extra source px above the stock crop). Adding a hair style / flag = new rect in the table + new entry in the option list; re-measure `HEAD_BOXES` only if the Character sheets change.

- **Spacecraft art (`src/spacecraft.js`, frames in `Assets/rocket/`):** the `Apollo Rocket_00NN.png` frames are each drawn at their own scale, so never place them by raw size. `CRAFT` holds a tight crop, a frame-absolute anchor (`ax`,`ay`, shared by every crop of one frame) and a relative scale `k` per sprite; always draw through `drawCraft(ctx, id, x, y, { scale, sx, sy })` (2px-grid snapped, flames stretch with `sy` from their nozzle) and `drawLaunchPad` (the tower's 4 swing arms are separate crops that fold via `retract[]`). On/off pairs (`stackIdle`/`stackBody`, `upperIdle`/`upperBody`, `command`/`commandBody`) share an anchor so engines can light without a jump. Used by Stage 1B (stack + pad + staging), 3A-3C (`lander`, split into `landerAscent`/`landerDescent`), Stage 4 (`chuteCapsule` + `chutes`, one frame cut at the cable ring) and all five transitions. Unused: frames 0014/0015 and the full sheet. Any new module must be added to the import map in `index.html` and the `?v=` number bumped (currently 18).

## Pixel-Art Visual Rules
- **Pixel renderer (`src/pixel.js`):** Every stage draws through the `ctx` from `createPixelRenderer`, a `560x310` buffer addressed in logical `1120x620` units (1 art pixel = 2 logical px) and blitted nearest-neighbour. Keep drawing in logical coordinates; do not call `getContext('2d')` on `#game` elsewhere.
- **What the renderer does for you:** text is drawn hi-res in the pixel font, emoji in canvas text become pixel icons, large images are pre-downscaled and alpha-hardened, gradients are posterised into hard bands, `lineWidth` snaps to whole art pixels, `roundRect` radii are clamped to 4. Prefer flat fills on the 2px grid (`Math.round(v / 2) * 2`) for new art; avoid `shadowBlur`, `filter`, and soft glows.
- **Font:** `Jersey 10` (single weight) for DOM and canvas. Canvas font sizes are auto-scaled 1.3x and weights are dropped, so keep authoring sizes like the existing code.
- **Icons (`src/icons.js`):** never put raw emoji in DOM templates; wrap HTML with `iconize(...)` or use `iconHtml('name')` / `data-icon="name"` in `index.html`. New symbols go in `RAW_ICONS` + `EMOJI_TO_ICON`.
- **DOM UI (`styles.css`):** no `border-radius`, blur, or soft shadows. Frames are notched 2px box-shadow outlines (`var(--frame)`, `--frame-hazard`, ...) with `var(--bevel)`; animations use `steps()`.
- **Crew sprites:** `drawChibiEngineer` in `src/sprites.js` draws `Assets/Character/Character_{Idle,Carry}.png`; frame mapping lives in `CREW_ANIM`. Roles are told apart by the ring under the feet and the name tag.
- **Debug:** `window.__orbitalPixelStats` exposes per-frame flush count and draw timings.

## 4-Stage Macro Level Architecture & Sub-Stages
1. **Stage 1 — Build & Launch (`src/stages/stage1.js`, `src/stages/stage1b.js`):**
   - **Stage 1A (Overcooked Assembly Hangar — Free Build):** Clickable order tickets with exact Crate-to-Bench component steps, a single top Active Target Recipe Bar with live step checkmarks, on-floor target station highlight rings, BFS grid-pathfinding AI squadmates, and a `+150⭐` full-stack integration bonus when all 4 core modules are installed.
   - **Stage 1B (Rocket Liftoff & Ascent):** Vertical thrust target band (`W`/`S`), horizontal pitch stabilizer (`A`/`D`), dynamic high-altitude Wind Shear gusts, and a precision Max-Q booster staging window (`SPACE`) with outward-peeling twin SRBs.
2. **Stage 2 — Inside the Rocket: Orbital Mission Control (`src/stages/stage2.js`):**
   - Among Us-style spacecraft control deck with a translunar trajectory holo-table, physical wrench rack, and 4 interactive console mini-games (Spectrometer Target Tracking, Eclipse Load-Shedding Breakers, Ka-Band Wave Alignment, Wrench Bolt Torque).
   - Unresolved console alerts actively drain Bus Battery & Ship Integrity (triggering a red emergency alarm vignette when 3+ alerts stack or systems drop critical), and Lunar Orbit insertion requires meeting the minimum task quota (`3` tasks) and clearing active alerts.
3. **Stage 3 — Moon Landing & Surface Exploration (`src/stages/stage3.js` — `3A` & `3B`):**
   - **Stage 3A (Powered Lunar Descent):** Tilted 3/4 perspective lunar plain with Y-depth sorting; powered touchdown on **Point A: Landing Pad** (`W`/`SPACE` retro-thrust, `A`/`D` lateral RCS). Hard or off-pad landings apply a `-28 Wh` starting rover battery shock penalty in `3B`.
   - **Stage 3B (Dual Moon Rover EVA):** Dual 6-wheeled rovers (`LTV-1` and `LTV-2`) with a live Lunar EVA Mission Checklist Card, off-screen directional waypoint arrows, field repair at the Point A Lander (`SPACE` to clear shock damage), 3 surface EVA tasks, and core sample drilling at **Point B: Target Crater** before returning to the rocket.
4. **Stage 4 — Lunar Launch & Earth Return (`src/stages/stage3.js` `3C`, `src/stages/stage4.js`, `src/stages/stage5.js`):**
   - **Stage 3C (Lunar Ascent & Cislunar TEI Burn):** Ascent Capsule launches vertically from the lunar descent stage (`W`/`SPACE` + `A`/`D` pitch corridor) and fires a timed Trans-Earth Injection (`TEI`) burn inside the green trajectory window (`progress 0.36..0.68`) to widen the Stage 4 Earth entry corridor.
   - **Stage 4 (Atmospheric Skip-Reentry & Carrier Recovery):** Guided atmospheric entry corridor (`W`/`S`), roll cooling (`A`/`D`), cryo heatshield venting (`SPACE`), altitude-gated Triple Main Chute deployment (`8–18 km` optimal window), and ocean carrier splashdown (`A`/`D` + `J`/`L`) guided by a visual Carrier Landing Zone Beacon.
   - **Stage 5 (Mission Debrief & Causal Black Box):** 4-pillar NASA grade breakdown (`S`/`A`/`B`/`C`), interactive telemetry replay timeline, and causal Black Box Flight Recorder linking every stage's choices to mission survival.
