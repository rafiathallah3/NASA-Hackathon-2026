How to Play Orbital Kitchen

Follow the Corner UI Spotlight Tour, assemble NASA payloads in the hangar, survive transit surges, explore 2.5D lunar cliffs, and return safely to Earth.

1. Hangar Build & Launch (Stage 1) Advance the Corner UI & Hangar Tours with SPACE or ENTER (TAB skips). Grab raw parts (SPACE), process on stations (Weld: hold SPACE, Calibrate / Charge: auto), plate onto a Tray, and deliver to Submit. Build at least 2 modules (aim for both REQ payloads) to unlock LAUNCH READY, then fly W/S, A/D, and SPACE at Max-Q.
2. Cruiser Systems & Surges (Stage 2) Navigate the 5 ship bays (WASD) and clear 4+ console alerts before arrival. Watch for Solar Flare / Micrometeoroid Surges that lock bulkhead doors—press SPACE at a locked door to override it, and grab the Wrench in Engineering to tighten Hull Breach bolts.
3. Moon Landing & 2.5D Cliffs (Stage 3) Touch down gently on Point A (WASD + SPACE). Drive rovers across High Cliffs (use ramps or SHIFT RCS Hop; sunlit plateaus recharge battery) and Low Cliff Basins (higher power draw; hold SHIFT to cushion drops), finish 3 EVA tasks, and drill Point B Crater before returning to Point A.
4. Lunar Liftoff & Earth Return (Stage 4) Launch from the Moon, fire SPACE inside the green TEI burn window, hold Earth entry pitch in green (W/S) and vent coolant (SPACE), open chutes at 8–18 km (SPACE), and steer (A/D + J/L) onto the recovery carrier.

Controls: WASD Move  •  SPACE Interact / Override  •  SHIFT Dash / RCS Hop  •  F Throw  •  1-4 Crew  •  ESC Pause
