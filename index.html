How to Play Orbital Kitchen

Build rocket modules in the hangar, fix ship alerts in flight, gather lunar samples, and bring the capsule home.

1. Hangar Build & Launch (Stage 1) Grab parts from left crates (SPACE). Process them on matching stations (Weld: hold SPACE, Calibrate / Charge: automatic). Place both parts on a Tray and deliver to Submit, then hold W/S, A/D, and SPACE at Max-Q liftoff.
2. Cruiser Systems (Stage 2) Walk between the five ship rooms (WASD) and clear at least 3 red console alerts before arrival. Grab the Wrench in the Engine Room before fixing Hull Breach bolts.
3. Moon Landing & Rovers (Stage 3) Steer with WASD and fire landing thrusters (SPACE) to land gently on Point A. Then drive the rover to scan ridges, complete 3 EVA tasks, and drill a core sample at Point B Crater before returning to Point A.
4. Lunar Liftoff & Earth Return (Stage 4) Launch from the Moon, hit SPACE inside the green TEI burn window, keep Earth entry pitch in green (W/S), open chutes between 8 and 18 km (SPACE), and steer (A/D + J/L) to the carrier.

Controls: WASD Move  •  SPACE Use / Pick / Place  •  SHIFT Dash  •  F Throw  •  1-4 Switch Crew
